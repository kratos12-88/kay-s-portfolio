FAQ

Questions that usually come up before a project starts.

What kind of work are you taking on?

Product websites, web applications, interface cleanup, QA-heavy product work and AI evaluation projects are the strongest fit.

Can you work with an existing codebase?

Yes. The first step is understanding the current stack, constraints and what is actually broken before changing the interface or architecture.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I’m based in Lagos and work remotely across software and AI-focused projects.

How do you price projects?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, timeline and whether the work is fixed-scope, sprint-based or ongoing. The inquiry form collects the information needed for a useful quote.

Can you handle deployment too?

Yes. Deployment support can be included when the project stack and hosting platform are within scope.
